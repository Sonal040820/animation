# STORM × ASH — ELITE CUT
**Original anime-inspired animated fight short | 23.0s | 1080×1920 | 24 fps | mono/stereo mixed AAC | No pre-existing characters**

## Story premise
On a storm-black rooftop, two former allies face each other at the end of a ruined city. KAI controls electric-blue kinetic force and refuses to surrender. REN uses ember-red impact energy and intends to break through him. Their rivalry is shown by restraint: every hit has a concrete cause, reaction, planted foot, shift of center of mass, and consequence. No blood or weapons.

**Theme:** The stronger fighter isn't the one who strikes first; it's the one who refuses to yield.

### Staging and screen direction
KAI begins at screen-left, facing screen-right. REN begins at screen-right, facing screen-left. Their crossing axis is never reversed without an establishing move. Camera begins low across the rooftop; the crimson eclipse is fixed behind the central fighting line. **Both fighters remain readable in medium/wide shots during choreography**; inserts only for dialogue, a footplant, and one moment of held contact. Blue is not synonymous with the winner.

## Acting and voice direction
- NARRATOR: low, almost whispered, no trailer-man shouting, neutral English; let consonants breathe.
- KAI: composed, restrained male voice; "Walk away, Ren" is a genuine last warning, not a threat; later question is an admission.
- REN: lower, dry and warm; "Make me" lands like a quiet dare; "So are you" has a small exhale before it.
- No character lines during contact frames. Do **not** subtitle fight sound effects.

## Physics / animation contract
1. Body trajectories originate in planted feet: 0.15–0.35 s anticipation, 0.10–0.28 s execution, ~0.30 s weighted recovery. No drift without cause.
2. Every punch uses hip rotation preceding shoulder then wrist. Hands pursue fixed targets; forearm/elbow hinge lengths are maintained using two-bone inverse kinematics.
3. Feet are planted until pressure shifts: reference ground plane y=1460; planted pose never causes an unexplained foot levitation.
4. Scarf and coat lag velocity changes and damp out; not synchronized to root joint motion.
5. Flight is brief and rare; dust fragments use parametric gravity y=y0+vy·t+0.5g·t².
6. 24fps output with intentional held drawings/on-twos for preparation and full on-ones/smears for dash; impact frames last one or two frames.
7. Motion blur and flash are physical-story emphasis, not constant. Camera shake is restricted to contact beats and decays after impact.
8. Light direction follows practical sources (eclipse backlight, cyan and crimson character energy). Strong value and silhouette separation.
9. Audio peak at most -1 dBFS (no digital clipping), dialogue upfront; duck soundtrack during dialogue.
10. Aim for stylized **2D procedural anime action**, not misrepresenting it as polished studio-frame-drawn sakuga or full rigid-body physics.
