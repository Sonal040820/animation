# STORM / ASH
- Format: vertical 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 12.0 seconds.
- Original anime-inspired cel-shaded silhouettes, no existing franchise characters.
- Original scripted digital instrumental score + action effects rendered as PCM WAV on GitHub runner.
- Beat sheet: 0.0–1.8 standoff and wind; 1.9–2.6 azure dash; 2.58 impact; 3.75 ash retaliation; 4.92 second impact; 6.24 charge; 7.15 sky-splitting final clash; 8.8 aftermath; 10.4 title.
- Color: blue/cyan (#45dcff), hot crimson (#ff5474), ink #060613 and violet storm #271640.
- Rig: two hand-designed vector characters with articulated front arms, cel-shadow edges and glowing eye highlights. Camera effects are time-seekable GSAP.
- Sound: no dialogue; score, wind/dash, two punch impacts, rising energy and main finishing impact. All sounds are procedurally authored for this project; no third-party licensed tracks.
- Caution: vector anime motion-graphic short, not photorealistic or fully frame-drawn 2D anime.
